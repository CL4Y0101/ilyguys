01 / THE LETTER

DEAR BYEONG YOON, JUEUN, SOO AH & DONGGYUN

Some chapters
are too short.

I honestly don't know how to say goodbye.

I thought this would simply be a project with students from Korea. A few days of working together, building something, taking some photos, then everyone goes home.

But somewhere between the awkward conversations, the jokes, the food, the project, and all those small moments, you became people I genuinely don't want to say goodbye to.

So I made this little place on the internet.

Not because a website can replace being together — it can't. But because I wanted there to be somewhere we can return to when these days start feeling far away.

— Aditya
